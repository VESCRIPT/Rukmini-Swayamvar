Child Safety Policy

Rukmini Swayamvar

1

Introduction

We are committed to providing a safe and secure matrimonial platform. Protecting users from child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) is our highest priority.
2

Age Restriction

Our platform is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 years and above. Users under 18 are strictly prohibited from registering or using our services.
3

Zero Tolerance Policy

We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against:

CSAM: Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
Exploitation: Any attempt to exploit minors
Verification: Fake profiles involving underage individuals
Conduct: Grooming, harassment, or inappropriate behavior

Any violation will result in immediate account suspension or permanent ban, and may be reported to law enforcement authorities.

4

Safety Measures

To ensure user safety, we implement:

Active monitoring of user profiles and activity
Moderation systems
Detection and removal of suspicious or fake accounts
5

Report and Block Feature

Users can report or block any profile directly within the app.

We provide reporting options including:

Harassment: Any form of verbal or behavioral harassment
Spam: Junk or irrelevant profile information
Fake Profiles & Scams: Fraudulent accounts or suspicious activities
Inappropriate Content: Explicit or offensive materials
Child Safety: Any child safety concerns
All reports are reviewed promptly by our team.
6

Action Against Violations

If violations are detected, we may:

Remove harmful or inappropriate content
Suspend or permanently ban accounts
Report serious violations to relevant law enforcement authorities
7

Legal Compliance

We comply with all applicable child protection, online safety, and cyber laws, and cooperate with law enforcement agencies when required.
