Rukmini Swayamvar

Welcome to Rukmini Swayamvar

Where Traditions Meet New Beginnings. Inspired by the sacred verses of Mahadamba (Mahadaisa), the first poetess of Marathi literature and a devoted disciple of Sarvajna Shri Chakradhar Swami, our platform is more than just a matrimonial site. It is a tribute to the Rukmini Swayamvar—a story of choosing a life partner based on devotion, values, and character.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help the Mahanubhav Panth community—also referred to as Jai Krishni Panth, Achyuta Panth (in Gujarat), or Paramarga—find life partners who share the same spiritual roots, vegetarian lifestyle, and commitment to the Panch Krishna philosophy. We blend modern technology with the sanctity of our 800-year-old heritage to create a safe space for your "Reshimgaathi" (sacred knots).

Why Choose Rukmini Swayamvar?

  • Spiritual Connection: Profiles are curated to match spiritual values and traditions
  • Community Focused: Built specifically for Mahanubhav Panth and related communities
  • Secure & Verified: All profiles are verified for authenticity
  • Heritage Preservation: Honoring 800 years of sacred traditions
  • Modern Technology: Latest technology to connect kindred souls
